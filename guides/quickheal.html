Lurkers.io Quickheal Tutorial

by AethericNebula

A tutorial teach you how to quickheal in Lurkers.

Information

Quickheal is a skill that makes you use medkits faster, it's frequently used in PvP or high-intensity battles. Using Quickheal is simple. First, make sure you have medkits in your hotbar.

Take a Medkit and drag it into the hotbar slot you are currently using, then use LMB click the screen quickly.

Tip: Quickheal may not work if you are holding an item that can already heal you, such as a Pumpkin or a Medkit.
