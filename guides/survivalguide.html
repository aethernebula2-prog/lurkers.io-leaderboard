Lurkers.io Survival Guide

by Finter Zains Cortisol Ameshyte

A simple guide on how to play Lurkers.io Survival, from your first resources to establishing your own base.

Phase 0: Beginning

  1. Go to Lurkers.io.
  2. Press Esc if the main menu UI does not appear.
  3. Press Servers on the left side of the UI.
  4. Find a low-player server with the gamemode Team Deathmatch or Bedwars.
  5. Complete the tutorial, or skip it if you want to.
    6. Key / Button Action
    W A S D Move
    LMB Break / Use
    RMB Place blocks / Open some menus
    B Open crafting menu
    C Drop selected item
    Mouse Wheel Change selected slot

    Learn to use the sword. Hold LMB to charge it, point your cursor where you want to slash, and release when it is preferably around 2/3 charged. You can also use the sword to boost yourself by pointing your cursor above you, jumping, and slashing shortly after.

    A useful sword movement technique is:

    1. Charge the sword.
    2. Aim your cursor above yourself.
    3. Jump.
    4. Shortly after jumping, release LMB and slash.
    Tip: Don't worry about being the best player yet. The goal at this stage is simply to learn how the game works.

    Get coins, fight enemies, and experiment with different weapons. You might die — that's completely fine.

    If you're playing on a team, help your teammates kill enemies or destroy the enemy crate depending on the gamemode.

    Remember: Don't quit just because your team is losing. You can still help your team and learn from the match.

Phase 1 — Materials

Gather Wood

  1. Find birch trunks.
  2. Gather around 10 birch trunks.
  3. Hold LMB while aiming at the trunk.
  4. Press B to open crafting.
  5. Convert birch trunks into wood.

Get Your Pickaxes

  1. Craft a wood pickaxe.
  2. Use it to mine 3 stone.
  3. Craft a stone pickaxe.
  4. Use it to gather more stone.

Get Basic Tools

Craft a stone axe.

  • Wood: 30–40
  • Stone: 30–40
  • Sword: 3 stone + 3 wood

Phase 2 — Preparation

You can kill other players for loot. This is optional and can be dangerous.

Get Iron

  1. Find iron.
  2. Mine 3 iron.
  3. Craft an iron pickaxe.

Iron Pickaxe: 3 iron + 3 wood

Collect More Resources

Search for more iron and gold. Aim for approximately 23–26 iron in total, plus some gold.

Other Supplies

  1. Kill people for loot. This is optional, and you may die in the process.
  2. Find iron and mine 3 iron.
  3. Craft an Iron Pickaxe using 3 iron and 3 wood.
  4. Find iron and gold. You will need around 23–26 iron in total.
  5. Get a Shotgun and Shotgun Ammo from a supply crate.
  6. Get Wheat Seeds if you don't already have better crops.
  7. Collect around 50 blocks of Dirt.

Phase 3 — Setting Up

Find a Hidden Location

Go approximately 200–300 blocks underground. Find or dig out a suitable underground area.

Build Your Base

  1. Use some stone to make bricks.
  2. Build a brick perimeter.
  3. A mildly horizontal rectangle works well.
  4. Create an entrance.
  5. Place a wooden door.
  6. Add a supply crate.
  7. Add a chest.

Create a Farm

  1. Place dirt horizontally in several layers.
  2. Leave approximately 2 blocks of separation between layers.
  3. Plant wheat seeds on the dirt.
  4. Wait for the wheat to grow.
  5. Harvest it.

Make Coins

  1. Craft some bread with wheat.
  2. Leave approximately half of your wheat unused.
  3. Open a supply crate with RMB.
  4. Sell wheat for coins.
  5. Harvest more wheat.
  6. Repeat.
Goal: Keep farming and selling wheat until you have enough coins for more medkits and potentially better guns.

Phase 4: Uprising

  1. Find a trustworthy player to help defend your base. This is optional, but having an ally can make things easier. You can make friends by helping other players with farming, building, mining, defending, and other tasks.
  2. Go deep into the mines and look for Mithril and other minerals. Try strip mining through large areas of stone, leaving 2 blocks of separation between each tunnel.
  3. Keep mining until you have enough materials for Mithril armor and tools, or until you are satisfied with what you have found.
  4. Get the armor pieces you need from other players. You can practice PvP in other gamemodes first if you want to.
  5. Once you have full Mithril armor, a good supply of medkits, enough money and materials, and a stable base, you have successfully established yourself.

From here, you can choose what to do next: help other players, fight, build and upgrade your base, create a peaceful community, teach new players, or keep your base going and build your own legacy.

You've Established Yourself!

  • Once you have full Mithril armor, Mithril tools, a decent supply of medkits, enough money and materials, a stable base, and reliable allies, you have successfully established yourself.

    • What happens next?

    From here, you can play however you want.

    • Help other players.
    • Fight other players.
    • Build and upgrade your base.
    • Create a peaceful community.
    • Explore and mine.
    • Build a long-lasting legacy.
    • Don't grief.

    Notes & Observations

    This guide was inspired by the experience of playing Lurkers.io since its release on Poki Games and by helping new players learn the game.

    Lurkers.io is a game where cooperation, exploration, building, combat, and simply experimenting can all become part of the experience.

    Help the community grow

    If you enjoy Lurkers.io, consider introducing someone else to the game. Teach them how to play, play together, and share this guide.
